FAQ

Frequently Asked

Is Prosepal free to use?

Yes! Try Prosepal with 1 free generation. Pro unlocks 500 messages per month for $4.99/month or $29.99/year — less than a greeting card costs.

What occasions does Prosepal support?

40 occasions including birthdays, thank you, sympathy, weddings, graduations, new baby, get well, anniversaries, retirement, holidays (Christmas, Hanukkah, Diwali, Eid, Lunar New Year), Mother's Day, Father's Day, Valentine's Day, and even pet occasions.

How is Prosepal different from ChatGPT?

Prosepal is purpose-built for greeting cards. No prompts to write — just select an occasion, relationship, and tone. You get 3 unique options instantly. It's designed for the card aisle, not generic text.

Will people know I used AI?

Only if you tell them. The messages aren't generic templates — they're crafted based on your specific inputs. You choose the tone, you pick from 3 options, you can edit before copying. The final message is yours.

What AI does Prosepal use?

Prosepal is built with Google Gemini, specifically optimized for heartfelt, personalized greeting card messages that understand relationship dynamics and emotional context.

Can I use Prosepal for Moonpig or Thortful cards?

Absolutely! Generate your message in Prosepal, then paste it into any card platform like Moonpig, Thortful, or even a physical card you bought at the store.

What to write in a sympathy card?

Sympathy cards are the hardest to write. Prosepal generates thoughtful condolence messages that express genuine care without being cliche. Just select 'Sympathy', your relationship, and a heartfelt tone.

What to write in a get well soon card?

Get well messages should be uplifting without minimizing illness. Prosepal crafts encouraging messages tailored to your relationship — whether for a friend, coworker, or family member.

What to write in a wedding card?

Wedding messages should celebrate the couple while feeling personal. Prosepal generates unique wishes based on your relationship — from heartfelt messages for close friends to appropriate well-wishes for coworkers.

What to write in a new home card?

New home cards should celebrate this exciting milestone. Prosepal generates warm housewarming messages — from funny quips about DIY projects to heartfelt wishes for making memories in their new space.
