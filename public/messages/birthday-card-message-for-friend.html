Birthday Card Messages for a Friend

Updated February 2026 • 4 min read

Your best friend's birthday is coming up, and "Happy Birthday!" just isn't going to cut it. This is someone who knows your coffee order, your deepest fears, and that embarrassing thing from high school. They deserve a card that matches the friendship. Here's how to write it.

Tips for Writing Birthday Messages for Best Friend

What to Keep in Mind

  • Inside Jokes Are Gold: The best birthday cards for friends reference your shared history. That inside joke that only you two understand? Include it. They'll love it.
  • Balance Humor and Heart: Start with something funny if that's your dynamic, but end with something real. Let them know they matter to you—you can be sincere and still be you.
  • Celebrate Them Specifically: Don't just wish them a happy birthday—tell them what makes them worth celebrating. Be specific about the qualities you love.

Short Birthday Messages

When you want something brief but meaningful:

"Happy birthday to my favorite person to do nothing with. Here's to another year."
"Another year of putting up with me. Thanks for that. Happy birthday!"
"You're the best friend a person could ask for. Happy birthday!"

Medium-Length Messages

For when you want to say a bit more:

"Happy birthday to the person who knows all my secrets and still sticks around. That's either friendship or blackmail material, and either way, I'm grateful for you."
"You're the friend I text when something good happens, something bad happens, or nothing happens at all. Basically, you're stuck with me. Happy birthday!"
"Another year older, another year of me being the younger friend. (You're welcome for that.) Happy birthday to my favorite person."

Heartfelt Messages

When you want to express deeper feelings:

"Happy birthday to the person who's been there through everything—the highs, the lows, and all the random Tuesday night texts. I don't know what I did to deserve a friend like you, but I'm not questioning it. Love you always."
"I don't say it enough, but you're one of the best things in my life. You show up, you listen, you make me laugh until I can't breathe. I hope this birthday is as amazing as you are. Love you."
"Here's to another year of adventures, inside jokes, and being the kind of friends that people make movies about. (We're definitely the funny ones, not the dramatic ones.) Happy birthday. I love you more than I probably say."
"You've seen me at my best and my worst, and you've chosen to stay for all of it. That's not nothing—that's everything. Happy birthday to my person. Here's to many more years of this."

When to Send Your Birthday Card

Make sure your card arrives on or before their birthday. If you're seeing them in person, bring it to the celebration. For long-distance friends, mail it a week early and follow up with a phone call or video chat on the actual day.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I'm bad at writing cards?

The fact that you're trying means something. Even a short, genuine message matters more than a perfectly crafted impersonal one. Start with one true thing you appreciate about them.

Can I be too cheesy?

For a best friend? Probably not. They know you. If cheese is your style, lean into it. If it's not, don't force it—be authentically you.

What about a long-distance friend?

A card in the mail means extra because you took the time to send it. Mention missing them and maybe suggest plans to see each other soon.
