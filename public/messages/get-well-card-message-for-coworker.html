Get Well Card Messages for a Coworker

Updated March 2026 • 4 min read

Your coworker is out sick or recovering, and you want to send something supportive. But how personal should you get? How do you acknowledge their situation without prying? Here's how to write a get well card that's warm without overstepping.

Tips for Writing Get Well Messages for Coworker

What to Keep in Mind

  • Keep It Light Unless You're Close: For casual coworker relationships, a warm but brief message is appropriate. Save the deeply personal notes for people you know well outside of work.
  • Don't Pry Into Their Situation: You don't need to know (or mention) specifics about their illness. "Hope you feel better soon" works for any situation.
  • Offer to Help With Work: One of the best things you can offer a recovering coworker is peace of mind about their workload. Let them know things are covered.

Short Get Well Messages

When you want something brief but meaningful:

"Hope you're feeling better soon! We miss you around here."
"Wishing you a speedy recovery. Take all the time you need."
"Get well soon! The office isn't the same without you."

Medium-Length Messages

For when you want to say a bit more:

"I hope you're resting and taking care of yourself. Don't worry about anything here—we've got it covered. Focus on getting better!"
"Wishing you a quick recovery! The team is thinking of you. Please don't hesitate to reach out if there's anything I can do."
"Heard you're under the weather and wanted to send some good vibes. Take all the time you need—we'll hold down the fort until you're back."

Heartfelt Messages

When you want to express deeper feelings:

"I know this hasn't been easy, and I just wanted you to know that we're all rooting for you. Focus on getting better—the rest can wait. We miss you and can't wait to see you back."
"Sending you strength and good thoughts during your recovery. You're missed around here, but your health comes first. Let me know if there's anything at all I can do."
"Recovery takes time, and I hope you're being patient with yourself. The work will be here when you're ready. For now, focus on you. We're all thinking of you."
"I know it's hard to be away, but please don't worry about anything here. Your health is what matters. We're all looking forward to seeing your smiling face again soon."

When to Send Your Get Well Card

Send your card when you learn of their illness or surgery—the sooner, the better. If multiple coworkers are signing a card, coordinate quickly so it arrives while your coworker is still recovering. For longer recoveries, a follow-up card or message can also be meaningful.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should the whole team sign one card?

A team card is great for general illnesses. For more serious or personal situations, individual cards from close coworkers may be more appropriate alongside a team card.

What if I don't know what they're going through?

That's okay—keep it general. "Hope you're feeling better soon" works regardless of the situation. You don't need details to express genuine care.

Is it appropriate to send flowers or gifts?

A team gift or flowers is nice for longer recoveries. Check with HR or close coworkers about preferences. Some people appreciate flowers; others prefer cards or meal deliveries.
