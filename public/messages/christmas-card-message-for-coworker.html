Christmas Card Messages for a Coworker

Updated March 2026 • 4 min read

Reviewed by Prosepal Editorial Team · Greeting card writing specialists

Work holiday cards can be tricky. You want to sound warm without being too personal. These examples help you strike the right tone for teammates, peers, and close coworkers.

Tips for Writing Christmas Messages for Coworker

What to Keep in Mind

  • Keep It Warm and Inclusive: Use wording that feels seasonal and kind, while respecting different traditions and preferences.
  • Acknowledge Their Contribution: Adding one line of appreciation for their work makes the message more meaningful.
  • Match the Relationship: For close coworkers you can be more personal; for broader teams keep it concise and professional.

Short Christmas Messages

When you want something brief but meaningful:

"Wishing you a joyful Christmas and a restful break."
"Merry Christmas. Thank you for being a great coworker this year."
"Warm holiday wishes to you and your family."

Medium-Length Messages

For when you want to say a bit more:

"Merry Christmas. Thank you for your support and teamwork this year. Wishing you a relaxing holiday season and a strong start to the new year."
"Wishing you a joyful Christmas and well-deserved rest over the holidays. It has been great working with you this year."
"Happy holidays and Merry Christmas. I appreciate your reliability and positive attitude all year long."

Heartfelt Messages

When you want to express deeper feelings:

"Merry Christmas to a coworker who consistently shows up with kindness and professionalism. Thank you for making work better for everyone around you."
"As the year closes, I just wanted to say how much I appreciate working with you. Wishing you peace, joy, and meaningful time with the people you love this Christmas."
"Thank you for your support, patience, and collaboration this year. Wishing you a Christmas season that is calm, joyful, and full of good moments."

When to Send Your Christmas Card

Send your Christmas card in early to mid-December so it arrives before office slowdowns or holiday leave.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I say Merry Christmas or Happy Holidays?

Use the language that fits your relationship and workplace norms. Inclusive wording is usually safest in broader team settings.

Can I include work appreciation in a holiday card?

Yes. A short thank-you line makes the message feel specific and genuine.

Is humor okay in a coworker holiday card?

Light humor is fine if you know they will appreciate it. Keep it respectful and workplace-appropriate.
