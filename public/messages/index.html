Message examples hub

Card message examples for the moments people search for

Use these examples as a starting point, then use Prosepal on iOS when you want wording tailored to the recipient, tone, and details.

Need something more personal? Prosepal creates three message options from your exact occasion, relationship, and tone.

Read guides
Sympathy

Sympathy card message

I am so sorry for your loss. I know there are no perfect words, but I hope you know how deeply loved and supported you are right now.

Tip: Keep it warm, brief, and specific to your relationship. Avoid explaining grief or reaching for a silver lining.

Birthday

Birthday card message

Happy birthday. I hope this year gives you more of the moments that make you feel like yourself: easy laughter, good people, and days worth remembering.

Tip: Add one detail only you would know to keep the message from sounding copied.

Thank You

Thank you card message

Thank you for showing up so thoughtfully. What you did made a real difference, and I am grateful for the care behind it.

Tip: Name the help, the impact, and your appreciation.

Wedding

Wedding card message

Wishing you both a marriage full of patience, laughter, and the kind of everyday love that makes ordinary days feel lucky.

Tip: Match the tone to how close you are to the couple.

Graduation

Graduation card message

You earned this moment. I hope you feel proud not only of the result, but of the persistence, late nights, and courage it took to get here.

Tip: Graduation notes work best when they celebrate effort, not just achievement.

Get Well

Get well card message

I hope each day brings a little more comfort and strength. No pressure to reply; I just wanted you to know I am thinking of you.

Tip: Offer support without creating another obligation for the person recovering.

New Baby

New baby card message

Welcome to the world, little one. Wishing your family soft days, steady sleep, and so many tiny moments you will want to remember.

Tip: Keep it celebratory and gentle; new parents are often tired and overwhelmed.

Retirement

Retirement card message

Congratulations on a career worth celebrating. I hope this next chapter brings rest, freedom, and the joy of choosing your own pace.

Tip: Mention the person's contribution if you know it; otherwise focus on the future.

Apology

Apology card message

I am sorry for how my actions affected you. I understand why it hurt, and I am committed to doing better from here.

Tip: Own the harm clearly. Do not make the recipient comfort you.

Encouragement

Encouragement card message

This is a hard stretch, but I hope you can see what I see: your resilience, your steadiness, and the way you keep going even when it is heavy.

Tip: Be sincere and grounded. Encouragement is stronger when it is not forced.
