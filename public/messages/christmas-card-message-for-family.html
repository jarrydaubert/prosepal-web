Christmas Card Messages for Family

Updated March 2026 • 4 min read

Reviewed by Prosepal Editorial Team · Greeting card writing specialists

Family Christmas cards can be heartfelt, funny, or both. The best messages sound like you and acknowledge your shared year together.

Tips for Writing Christmas Messages for Family

What to Keep in Mind

  • Reference Shared Moments: A quick nod to family traditions or memories makes your card feel personal.
  • Keep It Warm and Simple: Holiday messages do not need to be long. A few sincere lines are enough.
  • Close With Good Wishes: End with a clear wish for peace, joy, and time together in the new year.

Short Christmas Messages

When you want something brief but meaningful:

"Merry Christmas to the best family anyone could ask for."
"Wishing our family a joyful Christmas and peaceful New Year."
"So grateful for all of you. Merry Christmas."

Medium-Length Messages

For when you want to say a bit more:

"Merry Christmas, family. I am so grateful for our laughter, our traditions, and the way we always show up for each other."
"Wishing you all a Christmas filled with warmth, good food, and great memories. Love you all so much."
"No matter how busy life gets, this season reminds me how lucky I am to have this family. Merry Christmas to all of you."

Heartfelt Messages

When you want to express deeper feelings:

"Merry Christmas to the people who know me best and love me anyway. Thank you for being my home in every season."
"This year had its highs and lows, but having this family made everything easier. Wishing us all peace, joy, and health in the year ahead."
"Christmas always brings me back to what matters most: our family, our love, and the memories we keep making together."

When to Send Your Christmas Card

Mail Christmas cards in early to mid December so they arrive before the holiday rush. For in-person gatherings, bring cards to your celebration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I include a family update in the card?

Yes. Keep it short and positive, and avoid oversharing personal details.

Should I write the same message to everyone?

You can use a base message, then add one personal line for close relatives.

Is “Happy Holidays” better than “Merry Christmas”?

Use whichever fits your family and recipient preferences. Either can be thoughtful when sincere.
