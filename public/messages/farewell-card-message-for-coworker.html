Farewell Card Messages for a Coworker

Updated January 2026 • 4 min read

A coworker is leaving—whether for a new job, retirement, relocation, or something else. They've been part of your work life, maybe a big part, and now you need to find the words to send them off. Here's how to write a farewell message that's meaningful without being awkward.

Tips for Writing Farewell Messages for Coworker

What to Keep in Mind

  • Acknowledge What They Brought: What did this person contribute to the team or to your work experience? Be specific about their impact—it shows you noticed.
  • Keep It Appropriate to Your Relationship: Close colleagues get heartfelt messages. Casual acquaintances get warm but brief well-wishes. Match your message to the actual relationship.
  • Wish Them Well in What's Next: Farewell cards should look forward. Wherever they're going, wish them success and happiness in their next chapter.

Short Farewell Messages

When you want something brief but meaningful:

"Best of luck on your new adventure! You'll be missed."
"It's been great working with you. Wishing you all the best!"
"The office won't be the same without you. Good luck out there!"

Medium-Length Messages

For when you want to say a bit more:

"Congratulations on your new opportunity! It's been a pleasure working with you, and I know you'll do amazing things. Don't be a stranger—let's keep in touch."
"I'm sad to see you go, but excited for what's ahead for you. Thank you for being such a great colleague. Wishing you nothing but success!"
"Working with you has been one of the highlights of my time here. Thank you for your [specific quality—humor, expertise, support]. The team is losing someone special."

Heartfelt Messages

When you want to express deeper feelings:

"I'm not going to lie—I'm really going to miss you. You've made work better, and not just because of your [skill/contribution]—because of who you are. Thank you for being a great colleague and an even better person. Your next team is lucky to have you."
"Some coworkers become colleagues. Others become friends. You fell into that second category for me. I'm so happy for your next chapter, even if I'm selfishly sad to see you go. Let's make sure this isn't goodbye—just see you later."
"The best part of any job is the people, and you've been one of the best parts of this one. Thank you for the collaboration, the laughs, and for making this place feel less like work. I hope our paths cross again."
"I always knew this place couldn't keep you forever—you're meant for bigger things. But man, we're going to miss you. Thank you for everything you've brought to this team. Go crush it out there."

When to Send Your Farewell Card

Give your farewell card during the person's last week, ideally at their farewell gathering if there is one. If you're close, a personal card in addition to any group card is a nice touch. Connect on LinkedIn if you want to stay in touch professionally.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if they're leaving for a competitor?

Keep it gracious. Business is business, and their career choices are their own. Wish them well: "Exciting new chapter! Wishing you success in whatever comes next."

Should the whole team sign one card?

A team card is standard practice and appreciated. If you're particularly close, consider also giving a personal card with a more meaningful message.

What if I don't know them well?

Keep it brief and warm: "Best of luck in your new role! It was nice working with you." You don't need a deep relationship to offer genuine well-wishes.
