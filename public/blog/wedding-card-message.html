What to Write in a Wedding Card: 50+ Message Examples

Updated January 2026 • 7 min read

You found the perfect card and picked out a thoughtful gift. Now you're staring at the blank inside of a wedding card, and "Congratulations!" feels insufficient. What do you actually write?

The best wedding card messages are personal, positive, and appropriate for your relationship with the couple. Here's exactly what to write, whether they're your best friend, your cousin, or your coworker.

Quick Tips

  • Use both names if you know the couple equally
  • Keep it positive—now isn't the time for marriage advice or jokes about divorce
  • Reference something specific if you can (how they met, a shared memory)
  • Match the tone to your relationship—formal for colleagues, warm for close friends

Wedding Messages for Close Friends

You've seen their whole love story. Your message can reflect that.

"I've watched your love story unfold, and let me tell you—the ending (well, beginning!) is even better than I imagined. So happy for you both. Here's to a lifetime of adventures."
"Finally! I've been waiting for this day almost as long as you have. You two are perfect together, and I'm so honored to celebrate with you. Love you both."
"From the moment you told me about [partner], I knew this was different. Watching you find your person has been one of the great joys of our friendship. Congratulations, you two."
"To my favorite couple: thank you for letting me be part of this day. Your love gives me hope. Now go be disgustingly happy together forever."
"Remember when you were nervous about the first date? Look at you now. So proud of you, so happy for you. Cheers to forever!"

Wedding Messages for Family

Family weddings are emotional. Lean into that.

"Watching you get married today fills my heart in ways I can't quite put into words. You've found an amazing partner, and I couldn't be happier for you. Welcome to the family, [partner's name]!"
"To my [brother/sister/cousin]: I've known you your whole life, and seeing you this happy is the best gift. [Partner's name] is lucky to have you—and so are we. Congratulations!"
"Our family just got better. Welcome, [partner's name]! And to you, [name]—you did good. Really good. Love you both."
"I've watched you grow up, and now I'm watching you start this incredible new chapter. So proud of the person you've become. Wishing you all the happiness in the world."
"Marriage is a big deal. But looking at the two of you, I have zero doubts. You're going to be amazing. Congratulations!"

Wedding Messages for Coworkers

Warm but professional. You want to celebrate without oversharing.

"Congratulations on your wedding! Wishing you both a lifetime of happiness and a honeymoon with absolutely no work emails."
"So happy to celebrate this exciting day with you! May your marriage be filled with love, laughter, and the occasional sleeping in. Congratulations!"
"Congratulations to the happy couple! Wishing you a beautiful wedding day and an even more beautiful life together."
"It's been wonderful watching your excitement leading up to this day. Congratulations—you deserve all the happiness!"
"Wishing you a lifetime of love and partnership. Congratulations on your wedding!"

Funny Wedding Card Messages

Only if you know they'll appreciate the humor. When in doubt, stay sincere.

"Congratulations! Remember: a successful marriage is basically two people repeatedly asking 'what do you want for dinner?' until one of you dies. Best of luck!"
"You're officially stuck with each other. Couldn't happen to a better pair of weirdos. Congratulations!"
"Marriage tip: The secret is admitting you're wrong even when you're absolutely, definitely right. Congratulations!"
"I give this marriage a solid 10/10. Would attend the wedding again. Congratulations, you two!"
"Congratulations on finding someone who tolerates your nonsense! [Partner's name], you're a hero. Much love to you both."

Short Wedding Messages

Sometimes the card is small, or simplicity says it best.

"Wishing you a lifetime of love. Congratulations!"
"So happy for you both. Here's to forever!"
"Congratulations! You two are perfect together."
"Love, laughter, and happily ever after. Congratulations!"
"Cheers to love! Congratulations on your wedding day."
"Two of my favorite people, together forever. Congratulations!"

Religious Wedding Messages

If you know the couple shares your faith.

"May God bless your marriage with endless love and grace. Congratulations on this beautiful union."
"Wishing you a marriage built on faith, love, and commitment. May God guide you both as you start this journey together."
"What a blessing to witness your love! May your marriage be filled with God's grace and abundant joy."
"As you begin your life together, may your home be filled with love, faith, and laughter. Congratulations and God bless."

Wedding Messages When You're Only Close to One Person

You might not know the partner well. That's okay.

"[Name], seeing you this happy is wonderful. [Partner's name], thank you for being the reason. Wishing you both a beautiful life together."
"Congratulations to you both! [Name], you deserve every happiness, and I'm so glad you found it. Best wishes for your future together."
"[Name] has always been someone special, so I know [partner's name] must be pretty amazing too. Congratulations to you both!"
"To [Name] and [Partner]: Wishing you a lifetime of love, laughter, and happiness. Congratulations on your wedding day!"

What to Avoid

  • Jokes about getting cold feet or "ball and chain" humor
  • References to ex-partners or past relationships
  • Unsolicited marriage advice
  • Anything that hints at doubt about the relationship
  • "Don't screw this up" (even jokingly)
